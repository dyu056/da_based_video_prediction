## Interpretation

The baseline replication succeeded and produced a stable nontrivial predictor. Validation loss improved rapidly early in training and flattened after the low-20 epochs, while PCC continued to improve later. This suggests the checkpoint chosen by validation loss and the final checkpoint tell slightly different stories:

- the best-loss checkpoint is earlier and more conservative;
- the final checkpoint has lower RMSE and higher PCC but a worse loss value.

This means the report should be explicit about which checkpointing criterion is used for the official baseline comparison.
