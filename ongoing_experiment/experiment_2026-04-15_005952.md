## Interpretation

This is the first server-side result where the DA method improves both core quality metrics at the same time: lower RMSE and higher PCC than the same open-loop host. The compute overhead is still material, but it is much better than the local smoke result. That makes the current pipeline a valid foundation for the next round of selective-assimilation and observation-sparsity experiments.
