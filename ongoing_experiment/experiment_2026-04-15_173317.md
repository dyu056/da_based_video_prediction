## Interpretation

The rerun succeeded and now provides the true DA evolution across epochs rather than only a single final-evaluation point. The latent ETKF curve is consistently below the open-loop curve on RMSE for all evaluated epochs and is generally above the open-loop curve on PCC after the early training stage. The strongest DA checkpoint on this evaluation is epoch `28`, where DA improves both metrics over the same host checkpoint.
